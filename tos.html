Voiid Studios Studio Guidelines & Terms

Welcome to our official studio portal. By opening a commission ticket or utilizing our development services, you agree to everything stated on this website.

1. Client Intake & Proof of Funds

  • Proof Required: All clients must provide verifiable proof of funds (PoF) via group funds screenshots or approved transfer methods before any project scoping or code writing begins.
  • Scope Definition: Projects require a clear outline of deliverables. Any requested features outside the initial agreement will require a separate quote and timeframe.

2. Payment Structure & Roblox Tax

  • Upfront Commissions: Clients must cover the Roblox marketplace tax so the net amount agreed upon isn't taxed. The studio takes a 20% management cut, and 80% is distributed directly to the active developers.
  • Game Revenue Splits: For ongoing projects sharing game revenue, a minimum baseline is established where the studio retains 10%, developers split 20% passively, and 70% remains with the game owner for reinvestment, advertising, or scaling.

3. Workflow, Timelines & Human Well-being

Note on Developer Availability: Our developers are human beings. They require rest, can experience exhaustion, and operate on a strict one-project-at-a-time policy to ensure maximum code quality. Tickets are prioritized strictly by the date the ticket was made. For example, if someone has been waiting for 2 weeks, their ticket is prioritized higher than a ticket made 1 day ago. We will respond to your inquiries as quickly as possible.

4. General Rules of Conduct

  • Follow all Discord Terms of Service and Roblox Community Standards.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, spam, or malicious behavior in tickets or public channels.
  • Misuse of the ticket system for trolling or opening fake commissions without proof of funds will result in an immediate service ban.
